Operator guidance

Detection and OCR confidence are uncalibrated model scores, not verified accuracy. Fast image mode can miss small plates; use Detailed for additional search. Use a clear front or rear plate view. Small, blurred or angled plates may remain unreadable. Check recognised text against the crop; format matching does not prove a correct reading. Cameras require HTTPS or localhost. For CCTV, upload a recording or connect a capture device. Direct RTSP URLs require a video gateway.

Video results report the overall processing average, including decoding, plate detection, OCR and MP4 export. Detector p95 is shown separately. Exported readings use the clearest crop in each track; this is recording analysis, not a live latency guarantee. Results are available for one hour while the server is running.
